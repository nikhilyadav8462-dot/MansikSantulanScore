About the Project

Why we built it and what it does.

❓

Problem Statement

Students often miss the slow build-up of stress from long screen hours, poor sleep and low activity.

🎯

Objective

Give students a quick self-check of their well-being based on daily habits.

🤖

Solution

An ML regression model behind a web form that returns a score in seconds.

👩‍🎓

Target Users

School and college students, and institutions studying habit trends.

Limitations

The score is an estimate from self-reported habits, not a clinical assessment. Results depend on the training data.
